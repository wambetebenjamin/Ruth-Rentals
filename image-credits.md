# Image credits

Every photograph on Ruth Rentals comes from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which allows free commercial use without attribution. We credit the source anyway, because it is the neighbourly thing to do. No AI generated stock photography is used anywhere on this site.

Photographs were taken in Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria, South Africa, Mozambique and a small number of other countries. Where a photo was taken outside East Africa it is used for the equipment or the event itself, and listing copy describes the item, not the location. Testimonial and owner portraits are illustrative: the names in those profiles are demo content.
